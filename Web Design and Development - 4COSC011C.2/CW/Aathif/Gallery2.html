MEAL PROGRAMS

Offering free or subsidized meal programs, such as soup kitchens, community meals, and mobile food distribution services, to provide hot meals to those experiencing hunger.

SCHOOL FEEDING PROGRAMS

Implementing school feeding programs that offer nutritious meals to students, particularly in low-income areas, ensuring that children have access to regular meals and can concentrate better in school.

FOOD RESCUE PROGRAMS

Collaborating with food retailers, restaurants, and farmers to rescue surplus food that would otherwise go to waste and redistribute it to those in need through food banks and other distribution channels.

COMMUNITY GARDENS

Supporting community garden projects where residents can grow their own fruits and vegetables, fostering food self-sufficiency and promoting healthy eating habits.
